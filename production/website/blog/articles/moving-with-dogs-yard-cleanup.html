Moving With Dogs? Add Yard Cleanup to Your Checklist

Yard Care
September 12, 2026•2 min read

Moving with dogs creates two yard checklists: leaving the old space in good condition and making the new one ready for your household. Dog waste cleanup is easy to forget until moving day, when gates are open and the yard is full of people carrying boxes.

Treat the two properties as separate jobs

Write down what each yard needs. At the old home, identify the lawn, side yard, or dog run your pets used. At the new home, inspect the space before assuming it is ready for your dog.

A service arrangement does not automatically transfer from one address to another. The new property may have a different ZIP, yard size, number of sections, or route availability. Confirm coverage and a new service day before relying on a continued schedule.

Purge Pros serves eligible addresses across Central Indiana, including areas around Carmel and Westfield. A city name alone does not confirm coverage for a particular address.

Plan the old yard’s final cleanup

Choose a time after most outdoor items have been moved but before you hand over access. Empty lawn space is easier to inspect than a yard crowded with furniture and storage boxes.

Check beneath the former positions of play equipment, planters, and patio furniture. Those spots may reveal waste or debris that was previously hidden. If your dogs remain at the property after pickup, include a final check for new waste.

For a rental, review the cleanup responsibilities you agreed to with the property manager. A scoop visit cannot guarantee a deposit outcome or replace other move-out requirements.

Inspect the new yard before regular use

Look at fence continuity, gate latches, loose objects, and the surfaces your dog will use. Identify where waste pickup tools or a closed bin can be kept without blocking a path.

If previous occupants left buildup, describe it before requesting service. You do not need to guess how many dogs lived there; explain what you can see and which areas need attention.

Update account and access details together

Provide the correct service address, current contact information, dog count, and agreed gate instructions. Avoid leaving an old access code or property note attached to the new yard.

Tell the team when access to the old property ends and when the new arrangement can begin. A move date, quote request, and confirmed service appointment are separate pieces of information.

Keep the first week simple

Set aside bags, a scoop, and a place to store them where you can find them among the boxes. A small routine from day one is easier to maintain than a backlog after unpacking.

Check the new ZIP and build your quote. Share that you are moving so the team can review both the service details and the transition.
