Dog Poop Removal Cost in Indianapolis: What Shapes Your Quote

Service Guides
September 12, 2026•3 min read

Dog poop removal prices are easiest to compare when you know exactly what each visit includes. A starting price for one dog in a small backyard is not a quote for three dogs using the front, back, and side yards. For Indianapolis homeowners, the useful question is: what will my actual plan cost, and what could change the first visit?

Here is how to compare the details without getting lost in a headline offer.

Start with the recurring visit price

Purge Pros prices standard recurring plans by dog count, visit frequency, cleanup area, and selected yard sections. The price is per visit. It is not a flat monthly subscription, so a monthly budget depends on how many visits occur.

For example, one dog, up to ⅛ acre, and one service area starts at $19.99 per weekly visit. The equivalent starting twice-weekly option is $15.99 per visit. Twice weekly means two visits in a typical week; the lower individual visit price does not mean a lower total weekly bill.

Use the price tool with your own details before treating a starting example as your rate. Standard eligible prices are visible before you provide contact information.

Compare the same cleanup area

Count the areas your dogs actually use. A back lawn and a separate side run may need different selections from a backyard alone. Describe patios or other hard surfaces where pickup is needed rather than assuming that every surface on the property is included.

The entire parcel size is not automatically the service area. Your house, driveway, and areas excluded from cleanup should not inflate an estimate of the space to be searched. Our yard-size guide explains a practical way to make that distinction.

Understand the first cleanup separately

Eligible new recurring customers receive the initial scoop surcharge waiver: the entire first cleanup is charged at the approved recurring visit rate, with no additional-time charge under that offer. The visit itself is paid. Eligibility is checked against service history and approved scope, with one offer per customer or household.

Without that promotion, standard initial or restart cleanup includes the first 30 minutes at the quoted visit rate, then $1 per actual additional minute. Ordinary recurring maintenance stays at its quoted rate regardless of time spent. These are different situations, so ask which applies before agreeing to service.

A one-time cleanup has its own pricing: $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. It does not receive the new-recurring-customer offer. Current billing timing and complete conditions are explained in the service terms.

Ask what the visit delivers

Price comparisons should include the selected areas, collection and haul-away, access arrangements, communication, and what happens if something needs attention. Ask whether extra sections, unusual property conditions, or custom work change the scope.

A useful comparison note has five lines: dogs, cleanup area, yard sections, visits per week, and first-cleanup terms. Put the same information in every quote request. That makes different offers easier to evaluate fairly.

Check availability before planning around a day

A supported ZIP lets you build a request; it does not reserve a route slot. Purge Pros reviews the full address and route capacity before confirming service. Properties with 10 or more dogs, more than one acre of cleanup area, or commercial or kennel needs use the custom-estimate path.

Explore Indianapolis service, compare the cleanup options, and check your actual inputs. A clear scope and a clear first-visit explanation are more useful than the lowest number in an advertisement.
