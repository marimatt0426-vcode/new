Weekly or One-Time Dog Poop Cleanup: Which Fits Your Yard?

Service Guides
September 12, 2026•3 min read

The right cleanup plan depends on how your dogs use the yard and how much upkeep you want to handle between visits. For Indianapolis-area homeowners, the useful comparison is between a recurring maintenance schedule and a single cleanup—not a headline price without the yard details behind it.

Here is how to work out what fits your property before requesting service.

Start with the space that actually needs scooping

Walk through the outdoor areas your dogs use. A fenced backyard may be the whole job, but some homes also need a side run, front lawn or patio included. The total lot size is not necessarily the cleanup area: the house and areas that do not need scooping should not be counted as service space.

Make a note of your dog count, approximate cleanup area and each separate section you want serviced. Include hard surfaces where cleanup is needed and explain any gates or access instructions. These details help the requested plan reflect the work you actually want.

Choose recurring service for ongoing upkeep

Recurring scooping gives the yard a regular maintenance routine. Purge Pros offers weekly, twice-weekly and every-other-week options for eligible plans. Available schedules and prices depend on your dog count, cleanup area and selected sections.

Think about how heavily your dogs use the space, whether children or guests share the yard, and how much scooping you want to do yourself between visits. A more frequent schedule may suit a heavily used yard; the price tool shows the options available for your inputs.

A one-time cleanup can make sense when you want a single visit before using the yard again or plan to handle ongoing scooping yourself.

Know what happens on service day

Approved recurring visits include a methodical search of the selected areas, collection and haul-away of dog waste, arrival communication and a gate-closure photo after completion. Keep the agreed areas accessible and share pet or property instructions before the visit.
