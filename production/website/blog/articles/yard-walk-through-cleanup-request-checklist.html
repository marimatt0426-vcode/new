A Yard Walk-Through Checklist for an Accurate Cleanup Request

Service Guides
September 20, 2026•2 min read

You do not need a perfect drawing to explain a yard. A short walk from the entrance through the areas your dogs use can reveal the details that make a cleanup request accurate. It is often more helpful than choosing a size based only on the home’s real-estate listing.

Use this checklist before you start the price tool, or when an existing yard layout changes.

Begin where the team will enter

Stand outside the intended entrance and follow the route into the yard. Note the correct gate, any narrow path, an interior gate and the way each latch works. Check that the entrance can close securely after use.

You do not need to put private codes into a public note or photograph. Keep those details for the established service channel. Our gate checklist explains how to describe the entrance clearly.

Mark the areas that need searching

List each section where pickup is requested: back lawn, front lawn, side yard, dog run or a patio area. Then name any spaces that should be excluded. The team should not have to infer the service boundary from where a fence happens to run.

Estimate the combined outdoor cleanup area as honestly as you can. The house, driveway and unrelated sections are not automatically part of that estimate. Review yard size versus lot size if those figures are easy to confuse.

Identify surfaces and current conditions

Mention gravel, mulch, hard surfaces, long grass, heavy leaf cover or muddy areas that affect finding and collecting waste. A generic ‘backyard’ description can hide several different working conditions.

If waste has accumulated, say so plainly and estimate when the yard was last thoroughly picked up. That helps planning; it is separate from the ordinary recurring maintenance routine. Do not assume a photo of one clear section represents the condition of the whole property.

Note obstacles without turning them into extra services

Check for furniture, toys, hoses, stored materials and items blocking the gate. Decide which can be moved before service. Large-object removal, landscaping and repairs are not automatically included in pet waste pickup.

A simple photo can explain an unusual layout when the team requests it. Frame the relevant area and leave out people, vehicle plates and access information that do not help explain the work.

Make a short request summary

A useful summary says: current dogs, requested frequency, cleanup sections, approximate serviced area and anything unusual about access or condition. Read it once as if you had never visited the property. Does it tell you what is included and where to enter?

Explore what dog waste removal includes, then check your ZIP and enter your yard details. The team reviews the full address and scope before confirming a service day. A clear request makes that conversation easier.
