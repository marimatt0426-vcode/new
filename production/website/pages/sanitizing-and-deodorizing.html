Remove the waste.
Before any treatment.

The first and most important step is physically removing the pet waste. An optional follow-up treatment is considered only after the source material has been collected from the requested areas.

  • Waste removal addresses the material creating the immediate mess.

  • Weather, temperature, drainage, surfaces, water access, and product directions can affect eligibility.

This is not a promise to sterilize soil, eliminate every odor, or prevent disease. Product choice, application, contact time, and re-entry instructions must follow the label used for the service.

Product-based application

When WYSIWash is used.

WYSIWash is a chlorine-based outdoor animal-area treatment delivered through its compatible application system. Purge Pros uses it only when the property and requested scope are appropriate for a label-directed application.

  • Visible waste is removed before a treatment is considered.

Clear expectations

What to know before requesting treatment.

Not Soil Sterilization

Outdoor soil and grass are living, uncontrolled environments. No responsible service should promise a permanently sterile yard.

Not Guaranteed Illness Prevention

The service does not replace vaccination, veterinary care, parasite prevention, public-health advice, or proper pet-waste removal.

Not Universal Odor Elimination

Odor results depend on the source, saturation, drainage, weather, surfaces, and whether new waste or urine continues.

Not Appropriate Everywhere

Some properties, surfaces, temperatures, weather conditions, or access arrangements may not be eligible for treatment.

Before requesting treatment

Common questions.

Can I add this through the instant-price tool?

No. Treatment availability and pricing require a separate property and scope review. Text the team to ask whether it is currently available for your address.

Is the treatment automatically safe for every pet, plant, and surface?

No product should be described as universally safe in every circumstance. Eligibility, application, precautions, contact time, and re-entry instructions depend on the current label and site conditions. Follow every instruction provided for people, pets, plants, and treated areas.

Will it eliminate every yard odor?

Results vary. Persistent odor can come from ongoing urine exposure, deeply saturated materials, drainage, standing water, heat, or another source. Treatment may help with an eligible concern, but permanent or complete odor elimination is not guaranteed.

Does this guarantee protection from Parvo, Giardia, or other illnesses?

No. We do not promise illness prevention, diagnosis, or treatment. Product efficacy claims depend on the exact organism, surface, concentration, contact time, and current product label. Consult your veterinarian about vaccination, exposure, symptoms, and animal-health concerns.

How often would my property need treatment?

There is no responsible universal schedule. Frequency depends on the property, dog use, odor source, weather, surfaces, product directions, and your goals. We review those factors before recommending whether treatment makes sense.
