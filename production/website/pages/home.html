Questions before you start?

Clear answers before you request service.

Do I need to be home during service?

No. As long as the approved service areas are safely accessible and we have the instructions we need, you can continue with your day. We send a heads-up text before arrival and a gate-closure photo after the completed visit.

How do I know whether you service my address?

Start with the ZIP check in the price tool. If your ZIP is eligible, you can see available pricing and request service. Your recurring route day is confirmed after our team reviews the exact address and route capacity.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term contract or minimum number of visits is required. There is no cancellation fee or repayment of the waived initial scoop surcharge. Charges for service already provided remain due.

How does billing work?

Recurring service is pay per visit, with no time surcharge on scheduled recurring visits. Your card is kept securely through Housecall Pro and charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent. Nothing is charged by checking your price or submitting a route request.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per additional minute. The $89.99 base deposit is charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent; additional time is invoiced after completion. The new recurring-customer offer does not apply.
