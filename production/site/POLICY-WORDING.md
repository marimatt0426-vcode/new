# "No start-up fee": the rule and the wording (single source)

Public edition: the rule and the approved wording, word for word. The engine tests and the page checker pin sentences to this file; change nothing here without an explicit owner decision.

## The rule, in one place

| Case | What is true |
|---|---|
| New recurring customer, first cleanup | Billed at the regular per-visit price. Up to 120 minutes of cleanup included. No start-up fee, no first-visit surcharge. Once per household. Purge Pros checks service history before the first visit. |
| First cleanup likely to need more than 120 minutes | The customer is told before work starts and agrees the extra time first. Extra time is $1 per minute, the standing extra-time rate (confirmed by the owner on October 8). Nothing extra is billed without the customer's agreement. |
| Returning customer (ended service, came back) or history unclear | Restart cleanup: the regular per-visit price includes the first 30 minutes; time beyond that is $1 per minute, billed after the visit. Confirmed with the customer before they approve. |
| One-time cleanup | $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per minute. Priced separately from recurring service. |
| Every later scheduled visit | The agreed per-visit price, however long the visit takes. Never billed by the minute. |
| When the card is charged | When the on-the-way text goes out. Any extra minutes are invoiced after the visit. |
| Leaving | No minimum number of visits, no cancellation fee, nothing to pay back. |

## Wording for the pages

**Claim (seal, heading kicker, facts):** No start-up fee

**Lead under the heading:** New recurring customers pay no start-up fee. Your first cleanup costs the same as every visit after it, even when the yard has built up.

**"How the no-start-up fee works" (the titled explainer; never an FAQ entry):**

- **Starting recurring service?** Your first cleanup is billed at your regular per-visit price, with up to two hours (120 minutes) of cleanup included. Every scheduled visit after that is the same price.
- **What if my yard needs more than two hours?** Very few do. If yours looks like it will, we tell you before we start and agree the extra time with you first, at $1 per minute. Nothing extra is billed without your OK.
- **Coming back after a break?** The no-start-up-fee first cleanup is for households new to Purge Pros. A restart cleanup is your regular visit price for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per minute. We confirm it with you before you approve anything.
- **One-time cleanup?** $89.99 covers the first 30 minutes, then $1 per minute.
- **When you pay.** Your card is charged when we text that we are on the way. Any extra minutes are invoiced after the visit. Regular scheduled visits are never charged by the minute.
- **Stopping.** No minimum number of visits, no cancellation fee, nothing to pay back. Full details are in the Terms & Conditions.

**Small print under the explainer:** Once per household. We check service history before the first visit.

**Short forms for tight places (facts bar, cards, bar):** "No start-up fee for new recurring customers" · "First cleanup: your regular price, up to two hours included"
