The head of a card is a tinted band: a compass dial that shows where the group sits from the middle of Indianapolis (one gold spot; the tick is north), the side of town, the county, and a count. Bands are sky and gold by position (they alternate down a phone and make a checkerboard in the pairs) and never the tint of the section the cards stand on. The first card takes the whole row; the others pair up from 1100px, so keep an even number of them. Every row is 58px; when one card of a pair has fewer rows, end it with a quiet link row (li.areas-town--more, shown only while the cards are paired) so both finish on the same line.
