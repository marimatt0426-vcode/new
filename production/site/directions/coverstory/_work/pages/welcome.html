Quick answers

Before your first scoop

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the selected service areas are safely accessible and your current access notes are on the account, service can be completed without you at home.

What time will the technician arrive?

Your route day is consistent, but it is not a fixed appointment time. We send an en-route text when the technician is heading your way.

When will my card be charged?

Your card is charged when we text that we are on the way. Any extra minutes are invoiced after the visit: time past 30 minutes on a restart or one-time cleanup, or extra time you agreed to before a first cleanup that needs more than two hours. What you were charged up front counts toward the total; it is not added a second time.

Regular scheduled visits are never charged by the minute. There is no flat monthly bill.

Where do I add my card?

Through the secure link we send you by text, by email, or both, once your route day is confirmed. It comes through Housecall Pro, the system we use for scheduling, billing and your customer portal. This page does not collect payment information, and you should never send card details in a text reply.

If the link has not reached you, text us at (317) 961-5865.

Where is my customer portal?

Your portal invitation is sent separately by email. Check your spam or promotions folder if it is not in your main inbox, then save the link for future access.

What if access, weather, or my plans change?

Text the office as early as possible with gate, pet, property, or schedule changes. If weather affects the route, Purge Pros will communicate the updated plan.
