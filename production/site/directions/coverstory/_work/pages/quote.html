Dog waste removal price questions, answered

How do I know whether you service my address?

Start with the ZIP-code check when you build your price. If your ZIP is covered, you go on to your price and can send a request. Every community and ZIP code we cover is on the service areas page.

Because recurring service is route-based, our local team still reviews the full address and current route capacity before confirming your service day. We would rather confirm a dependable route than promise a day we cannot support.

What happens after I request recurring service?

Sending your request asks for service. We confirm your route day before anything is booked. Our local team reviews your address, selected plan, and current route capacity.

After your route is approved and a service day is confirmed, we send your service details and a secure Housecall Pro card-on-file link. We contact you using the preference you chose when you built your price. The Yard Guide walks through it: from price check to confirmed service.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. There is no minimum number of visits and no cancellation fee. You pay for completed visits, and there is nothing to pay back if you stop.

Scheduled recurring visits stay at your quoted rate regardless of cleanup time. After a long pause or repeated customer-requested skips, a restart cleanup may be needed: your quoted visit rate includes the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. We confirm any restart pricing with you before you approve anything.

How do billing and payment work?

Recurring service is pay per visit, not a flat monthly bill. After route approval, you receive a secure link to place a major credit or debit card on file.

Your card is charged when we text that we are on the way. Any extra minutes are invoiced after the visit: time past 30 minutes on a restart or one-time cleanup, or extra time you agreed to before a first cleanup that needs more than two hours. What you were charged up front counts toward the total; it is not added a second time.

Regular scheduled visits are never charged by the minute. Receipts are provided electronically. We do not accept cash or checks left at the property.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. Choose One-time cleanup when you build your price to request service.

The $89.99 is charged when we text that we are on the way. Any additional time is invoiced after the visit. The $89.99 is credited toward the total, not charged again.

One-time cleanups are priced on their own, separately from recurring service.

What yard size should I enter for my quote?

Estimate the total outdoor area that needs scooping, including side yards, dog runs, and patios or concrete where your dogs go. Leave out the house and areas that do not need scooping.

Pricing uses dog count, visit frequency and the cleanup area you choose. If that area exceeds one acre or the work falls outside standard options, ask for a custom quote. A larger property does not automatically need a custom quote if the area needing scooping is smaller.
