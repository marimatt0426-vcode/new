Questions before you start?

Clear answers before you request service

Do I need to be home during service?

No. As long as the approved service areas are safely accessible and we have the instructions we need, you can continue with your day. We send a heads-up text before arrival and a gate-closure photo after the completed visit.

How do I know whether you service my address?

Your ZIP is the second question when you build your price. If your ZIP is eligible, you can see available pricing and request service. Your recurring route day is confirmed after our team reviews the exact address and route capacity.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term contract or minimum number of visits is required. There is no cancellation fee, and nothing to pay back if you stop. Charges for service already provided remain due.

How does billing work?

You pay per visit. Regular scheduled visits are never charged by the minute. Your card is kept securely through Housecall Pro and charged when we text that we are on the way. Nothing is charged for checking your price or submitting a route request.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per additional minute. The $89.99 base is charged when we text that we are on the way; additional time is invoiced after the visit. One-time cleanups are priced on their own, separately from recurring service.
