Questions before you start?

Brownsburg answers for a yard playing catch-up

What if a Brownsburg yard has not been cleaned recently?

Tell us roughly when the yard was last cleaned, so we can plan the visit. If you are starting recurring service, your first cleanup is billed at your regular per-visit price, with up to two hours (120 minutes) of cleanup included, even when the yard has built up. See how no start-up fee works for longer cleanups and restarts.

Is a one-time Brownsburg cleanup priced like recurring service?

No. One-time cleanups are priced on their own, separately from recurring service. A one-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per additional minute.

The $89.99 is charged when we text that we are on the way; any additional time is invoiced after the visit. The $89.99 counts toward the total and is not charged again. You can request a one-time cleanup when you build your price.

Does a Brownsburg ZIP confirm a recurring service day?

No. A covered ZIP lets you build your price. Before we propose a recurring day, we check your exact address, the areas you picked, and whether that route has room.

What should I include in my request?

Tell us how many dogs use the yard, roughly how big the area to scoop is, every front, back, side, patio, or concrete area that needs service, how often you want us, and how we get through the gate.

What starting prices should I compare?

For one dog, one service area, and a yard up to one-eighth acre, weekly service starts at $19.99 per visit. Twice a week starts at $15.99 per visit for the same yard. Your dogs, your yard size, and the areas you pick set your price, or send it to a custom quote.

What is included with recurring dog waste removal?

Every recurring visit includes a careful search of the areas you chose, all the dog waste we collect hauled away, tools sanitized between yards, a text when we are on the way, and a photo of your closed gate when we leave.

How is the per-visit price calculated?

Your price comes from four things: how many dogs, how often we visit, the size of the yard, and the areas you choose, counting any patio or concrete that needs scooping. Leave out the house and anything that does not need scooping. Larger or more complicated properties may need a custom quote instead of an instant price.
