C8 Level step titlesol.steps.steps--3.company-steps · ol.steps.steps--4.company-stepsA class for the shared steps on a company page. From 900px a row of steps is as tall as its longest step, and the shared step shares the spare height out between its rows, so the title of a step with a shorter text drops below its neighbours (16px on the welcome page at 1440; the same shows on the homepage at 1024). With .company-steps the spare height goes under the text and every title in the row stays level.
