How the no-start-up fee worksFirst cleanup: your regular price, up to two hours included
Starting recurring service?
Your first cleanup is billed at your regular per-visit price, with up to two hours (120 minutes) of cleanup included. Every scheduled visit after that is the same price.
What if my yard needs more than two hours?
Very few do. If yours looks like it will, we tell you before we start and agree the extra time with you first, at $1 per minute. Nothing extra is billed without your OK.
Coming back after a break?
The no-start-up-fee first cleanup is for households new to Purge Pros. A restart cleanup is your regular visit price for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per minute. We confirm it with you before you approve anything.
One-time cleanup?
$89.99 covers the first 30 minutes, then $1 per minute.
When you pay.
Your card is charged when we text that we are on the way. Any extra minutes are invoiced after the visit. Regular scheduled visits are never charged by the minute.
Stopping.
No minimum number of visits, no cancellation fee, nothing to pay back. Full details are in the Terms & Conditions.
