Helpful Answers Before You Start

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, scheduling, payments, pets, weather, and recurring service.

? Do I need to be home during service?

No. As long as the serviced areas are safely accessible and we have the access instructions we need, you can continue with your day.

We send a heads-up text before arrival and a gate-closure photo after the completed visit. Please leave the gate accessible or provide an approved access method, and follow any pet instructions discussed with our team.

? How do I know whether you service my address?

Start with the ZIP-code check in our online price tool. If your ZIP is currently covered, you can continue through pricing and submit a service request.

Because recurring service is route-based, we review the full address before confirming your service day. If we cannot currently fit the address into an active route, we will let you know rather than promising a day we cannot reliably support.

? What happens after I request recurring service?

Our local team reviews your address and available route capacity. After we approve and confirm the recurring route day, we send your service details and a secure link to place a payment card on file.

Completing that secure account setup prepares the account for your first confirmed service visit. You will receive service communication by your selected contact method.

? Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term contract is required. Recurring service is billed per visit and continues on the confirmed schedule until you ask us to change or cancel it.

Your recurring price is based on the selected service frequency and normal accumulation between visits. If a lengthy pause or repeated skips create substantially more work, the return visit may require review and adjusted cleanup pricing. We will explain any adjustment before charging it.

i The separate initial-cleanup-fee waiver applies to eligible new recurring customers. Your regular per-visit rate still applies on visit #1, and the waiver does not apply to one-time cleanups.
? How do billing and payment work?

You pay per visit, not through a flat monthly service bill. After route approval, you use our secure link to place a major credit or debit card on file.

For each scheduled visit, your card is charged when the ETA or on-my-way notification is sent, shortly before service begins. A receipt is provided through the service system. We do not accept cash or checks left at the property.

? Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanups are available separately from recurring service and use their own time-based pricing. Choose the one-time option in the online price tool to see the current terms for your request.

The new recurring-customer initial-cleanup-fee waiver does not apply to one-time cleanups.

? Are you insured, and who performs the service?

Purge Pros carries business insurance, and field service is performed by background-checked W-2 employees trained to our service, sanitation, gate, and property-care standards.

You will receive a service notification before arrival, and your service professional will arrive identifiable as part of the Purge Pros team.

? How do you handle pets, gates, and property access?
  • ✓We save the pet, access, and gate instructions you provide for the service team.
  • ✓Treats are offered only with your approval, and allergy, dietary, or no-treat preferences should be included in your notes.
  • ✓Tools and footwear are treated between customer yards as part of our sanitation process.
  • ✓We send a gate-closure photo after each completed visit.

If a dog is reactive, protective, uncomfortable with visitors, or likely to leave through an open gate, please keep the dog securely inside during service. Our team may pause a visit when conditions do not appear safe.

? Do you provide service year-round and during bad weather?

Yes, recurring service operates year-round. We generally work through ordinary rain, cold, and snow when the property can be serviced safely.

Lightning, flooding, unsafe ice, extreme cold, or another hazardous condition may require us to delay or pause service. We will send an update and attempt to reschedule based on safe conditions and route availability.

i If Purge Pros cancels because of hazardous conditions and cannot safely reschedule, you are not charged for that visit. The accumulated waste is addressed at the next scheduled visit without an additional accumulation charge.
? Can I skip, pause, or reschedule a service visit?

Please contact us as early as possible—preferably at least 48 hours before your route day. We will do our best to accommodate a change, but route-based scheduling means another day that same week may not always be available.

If your dogs will be away and no new waste will accumulate, tell us when requesting the pause. If dogs remain home, a skipped visit can create more accumulation than the recurring rate covers. A return visit may require review and adjusted cleanup pricing; any adjustment will be explained before it is charged.

A weather cancellation initiated by Purge Pros is not treated as a customer-requested skip.
