Quick answers

Before your first scoop.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the selected service areas are safely accessible and your current access notes are on the account, service can be completed without you at home.

What time will the technician arrive?

Your route day is consistent, but it is not a fixed appointment time. We send an en-route text when the technician is heading your way, generally 10–45 minutes before arrival.

When will my card be charged?

You are charged per visit when the en-route text is sent. There is no flat monthly bill.

Where do I add my card?

Use the private Housecall Pro card request sent separately by Purge Pros. This page does not collect payment information, and you should never send card details in a text reply.

Where is my customer portal?

Your portal invitation is sent separately by email. Check spam or promotions if it is not in your main inbox, then save the link for future access.

What if access, weather, or my plans change?

Text the office as early as possible with gate, pet, property, or schedule changes. If weather affects the route, Purge Pros will communicate the updated plan.
