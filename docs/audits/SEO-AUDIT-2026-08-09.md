# Purge Pros SEO audit

**Audit date:** August 9, 2026

**Website:** <https://itspurgepros.com/>

**Scope:** Live crawlability, metadata, canonicalization, structured data, URL architecture, representative city pages, local entity consistency, content, internal links, sampled competitors, and performance risks

## Executive conclusion

The redesigned site is visually strong, conversion-focused, and contains better service detail than many sampled local competitors. It is not yet technically positioned to compete reliably for the top organic and local results because Google is receiving incomplete, conflicting, and stale signals about the preferred pages and business entity.

The highest priorities are the empty sitemap, duplicate/legacy URLs, missing core-page metadata and canonicals, and the Trustindex markup that still identifies the company as `Purge Pros Junk Removal`.

No responsible SEO review can guarantee position one. Local results also vary by searcher location because Google evaluates relevance, distance, and prominence.

## Confirmed strengths

- Strong homepage title: `Dog Waste Removal Indianapolis | Purge Pros`
- Clear service differentiation: waived initial-cleanup fee for qualifying recurring customers, gate photos, text notifications, sanitized equipment, and collected waste hauled away
- Professional quote and service-request experience
- Dedicated city pages with localized titles, descriptions, canonicals, images, and substantial copy
- Correct HTTP-to-HTTPS and www-to-non-www redirects
- Real 404 responses for missing pages
- Useful city-to-city internal links
- Genuine review content
- Good custom `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, breadcrumb, and FAQ structured-data foundations

## Critical technical findings

### 1. Empty XML sitemap

`https://itspurgepros.com/sitemap.xml` returned HTTP 200 with an empty `<urlset>`. `robots.txt` was blank. A blank robots file is permissive, but it does not advertise the sitemap.

Recommended robots content:

```text
User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://itspurgepros.com/sitemap.xml
```

The sitemap should contain only fully qualified canonical URLs that should appear in search results. Submit it in Google Search Console after publishing.

### 2. Duplicate, obsolete, and broken URLs

Add permanent redirects:

| Existing URL | Redirect destination | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `/home` | `/` | Duplicate homepage; the navigation currently links to it |
| `/services` | `/dog-waste-removal` | Older duplicate service page |
| `/getquote` | `/?open_quote=1` | Legacy quote funnel and outdated offer language |
| `/additional-service-areas` | `/additional` | Footer target returns 404 |
| `/terms-and-conditions` | `/terms-conditions` | Previous legal URL returns 404 |
| `/testimonials` | `/reviews` | Preserve any old links or citations |

Update the navigation Home link to `/` and the footer service-area link to `/additional`.

### 3. Missing metadata and canonicals

The city pages generally have unique metadata and self-referencing canonicals. Several core pages do not.

| Page | Required work |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Add a standard meta description and complete social metadata |
| `/dog-waste-removal` | Unique title, meta description, canonical, and social metadata |
| `/sanitizing-and-deodorizing` | Unique title, meta description, canonical, and social metadata |
| `/about` | Meta description, canonical, and complete social metadata |
| `/reviews` | Unique title, meta description, canonical, and social metadata |
| `/faq` | Meta description and canonical |
| `/terms-conditions` | Document title and canonical |
| `/privacy-policy` | Document title and canonical |

Suggested titles:

| Page | Suggested title |
|---|---|
| Dog Waste Removal | `Dog Poop Removal Services Indianapolis | Purge Pros` |

### 4. Conflicting Trustindex structured data

The embedded Trustindex widget injects `Product` structured data naming the business `Purge Pros Junk Removal`. That conflicts with the custom Purge Pros local-business entity and current pet-waste-removal positioning.

Actions:

1. Rename the Trustindex profile to `Purge Pros`.
2. Change its category and description to pet/dog waste removal.
3. Disable Trustindex-generated schema if the setting is available.
4. Retain the visual review widget.
5. Publish one accurate authoritative custom business entity.
6. Validate representative pages with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator.

### 5. Business-entity inconsistency

Public sources still associate the domain and primary phone with the prior junk-removal business. Older site content also exposed two phone numbers.

Choose one primary public phone number and use it consistently in:

- Header and footer
- Contact and legal pages
- Custom structured data
- Google Business Profile
- Trustindex
- BBB and major directories
- Social profiles

The secondary line can remain operational, but it should not compete as a second primary NAP signal.

If customers cannot visit a permanently staffed, signed location, configure Google Business Profile as a service-area business and hide the address. Do not present a mailbox or unstaffed virtual office as a storefront.

## City-page assessment

The city pages have strong foundations:

- Localized titles and descriptions
- Self-referencing canonicals
- Targeted H1 headings
- County and city references
- Roughly 900 words per page in the sampled set
- Neighboring-city links
- Different hero imagery

After replacing city names, sampled Indianapolis, Fishers, and Carmel pages were approximately 76% similar by five-word phrase structure. This is not automatically a penalty, but new location pages should not be created by merely changing place names.

Strengthen the highest-value city pages with:

- Actual neighborhoods and ZIP codes served
- A city-specific customer review
- An authentic job photo or short case study
- Local route or seasonal considerations
- A unique FAQ
- Accurate neighboring-service-area links
- Clear route-review language rather than claiming every address is automatically eligible

Suggested improvement order:

1. Indianapolis
2. Fishers
3. Carmel
4. Noblesville
5. Greenwood
6. Westfield
7. Lawrence
8. Greenfield

## Local SEO and authority

The website is stronger than the current external entity footprint. Sustainable local improvement requires:

- Continued legitimate Google reviews
- GBP job photos and complete service listings
- Accurate service areas and operating information
- Local links from veterinarians, groomers, dog daycares, rescues, apartments, HOAs, chambers, and neighborhood organizations
- Sponsorships and local community coverage
- Cleanup of prominent old junk-removal citations

Reviews should be natural. Do not script customers to repeat exact city/service keywords.

## Content opportunities

Publish a small number of useful local resources rather than dozens of interchangeable posts:

- Dog poop removal cost in Indianapolis
- Weekly versus biweekly service
- What happens during the first cleanup
- Recurring versus one-time cleanup
- Winter dog-waste removal in Indianapolis
- How frequently a yard should be scooped
- Dog-waste risks for lawns, children, and pets using authoritative veterinary/environmental references
- What happens when dogs are in the yard during service

Each resource should link naturally to the primary service page, relevant city pages, and quote experience.

## Performance risks to measure

Google PageSpeed Insights was rate-limited during this audit, so no Lighthouse score is recorded here.

Rendered-page inspection identified these measurement priorities:

- Do not lazy-load the above-the-fold logo or primary hero asset.
- Prefer responsive WebP/AVIF `<picture>`/`<img>` elements over CSS-only hero backgrounds where practical.
- Give the hero explicit dimensions and `fetchpriority="high"` when it is the LCP candidate.
- Lazy-load the full Trustindex review wall until it approaches the viewport.
- Audit GoHighLevel and third-party scripts not required for the first render.
- Prevent widgets from shifting the page after load.

Capture mobile and desktop Lighthouse data before and after performance changes.

## Implementation sequence

### First 48 hours

1. Populate and submit the sitemap.
2. Add the sitemap declaration to robots.txt.
3. Add the six redirects.
4. Repair internal navigation/footer targets.
5. Add titles, descriptions, and canonicals to core pages.
6. Correct or disable Trustindex-generated schema.
7. Standardize business name and primary phone.
8. Use Search Console URL Inspection on the homepage, primary service pages, Indianapolis, Fishers, Carmel, Reviews, and About.

### Next two weeks

1. Improve the top city pages with real local proof.
2. Clean up major legacy citations.
3. Complete and verify the Google Business Profile configuration.
4. Add accurate Service schema to both service pages.
5. Add logo, image, `sameAs`, hours, and service-area information to the primary business entity.
6. Earn several relevant local links or partnerships.
7. Capture Search Console and PageSpeed baselines.

### Ongoing

- Publish one useful local resource at a sustainable pace.
- Continue collecting legitimate reviews.
- Add authentic service photos to the site and GBP.
- Measure organic leads by landing page and city.
- Use geographic rank tracking rather than relying on one desktop search.

## Measurement framework

Track:

- Google Search Console non-branded clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position by query and landing page
- Submitted canonical URLs versus indexed canonical URLs
- Google Business Profile calls, website clicks, and other available interactions
- Organic accepted service requests and Won customers
- Cost/revenue by organic landing page where attribution permits
- Core Web Vitals pass rate
- City-page performance over time
