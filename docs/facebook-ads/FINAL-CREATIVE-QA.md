## Visual checks completed

- Square high-resolution PNG output
- One clear dog-centered focal point per image
- Dog anatomy, visible paws, eyes, ears, nose, collar, fences, houses, trees, patios, lighting, and perspective reviewed at full resolution
- No generated service equipment, technicians, gates, hands, review counts, star ratings, dollar values, or guarantee seals
- Purge Pros logo checked for legibility
- Copy does not cover the dog's face or important visual evidence

## Deliberate exclusions

- No starting-price claim because the customer's price depends on service selections.
- No review count or rating because those figures change.
- No compressed `100% guarantee` badge without complete terms.
- No implication that one-time cleanups qualify for the waived initial-cleanup fee.
- Drafts, rejected gate concepts, and superseded image versions are not part of the production ad package.
