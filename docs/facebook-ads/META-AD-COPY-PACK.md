## Controlled-test rules

- Use the primary text above for the first comparison.
- Keep the audience, optimization event, destination, placements, and attribution settings identical.
- Do not add changing review counts, dollar starting prices, vague guarantee seals, or unqualified savings claims.
- Keep automated text generation and AI creative variations off for this controlled first test.
- Optimize for Meta's standard `Lead` event, not the retired `Quote Unlocked` action.
