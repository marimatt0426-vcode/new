# September 2026 website fragments (history)

The 27 GHL body fragments, their metadata and the global header and footer of the September 21, 2026 website rollout, with the September 23 navigation, Welcome and FAQ corrections. They were `production/website/pages/` and `production/website/shared/header.html` and `footer.html` until October 10, 2026, when the rebuilt website (v5) source took those places.

On October 10, 2026 this was still the website that is live: moving the files here records what the repository's `production/` folder describes, not a change in GHL. If the rebuilt website is not installed, or is rolled back, these are the fragments to restore from.

`pages/terms-conditions.html` and `pages/privacy-policy.html` are the Terms and the Privacy Policy as installed in September 2026 (the text last changed on September 9, 2026): the copy of the superseded text to keep once the rebuilt pages are published.

The September site head and body code stay in `production/website/shared/head.html` and `body.html`, because the rebuilt site's tracking tags are copied from them.

Not a deployment source for new work.
