Quick answers

Before your first scoop.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the selected service areas are safely accessible and your current access notes are on the account, service can be completed without you at home.

What time will the technician arrive?

Your route day is consistent, but it is not a fixed appointment time. We send an en-route text when the technician is heading your way, generally 10–45 minutes before arrival.

How does my first-cleanup offer work?

If your service confirmation includes the new recurring-customer offer, you pay only your quoted regular visit rate for the entire first cleanup, including built-up poop and all extra time. Following scheduled recurring visits also stay at your quoted rate, regardless of time spent.

Without the offer, standard initial/restart pricing includes 30 minutes at your visit rate, then $1 per actual additional minute. A 60-minute initial cleanup would cost your visit rate plus $30; the offer saves that $30. Actual savings vary: a cleanup of 30 minutes or less has no time surcharge to waive.

One introductory offer per customer/household, for new recurring service only. We confirm service history and any custom scope before booking. One-time cleanups excluded. No minimum visits or cancellation fee, and cancelling does not trigger repayment of the waived surcharge. Completed visits remain payable.

What if I booked a standard initial, restart or one-time cleanup?

A standard initial or restart cleanup includes the first 30 minutes at your quoted recurring visit rate, then $1 per actual additional minute. This replaces the maintenance charge for that cleanup; a second visit charge is not added.

One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. The $89.99 base deposit counts toward the total. The new recurring-customer offer does not apply.

The applicable base is charged with your on-the-way ETA text; any extra time is invoiced after completion. We confirm the applicable pricing before booking. Ordinary scheduled recurring visits have no time surcharge.

When will my card be charged?

Your visit rate or applicable base deposit is charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent. For standard initial/restart or one-time cleanups, any applicable extra time is invoiced after completion. The base counts toward the total, not a second charge. Approved promotional first cleanups and scheduled recurring visits have no additional-time charge. There is no flat monthly bill.

Where do I add my card?

Use the private Housecall Pro card request sent separately by Purge Pros. This page does not collect payment information, and you should never send card details in a text reply.

Where is my customer portal?

Your portal invitation is sent separately by email. Check spam or promotions if it is not in your main inbox, then save the link for future access.

What if access, weather, or my plans change?

Text the office as early as possible with gate, pet, property, or schedule changes. If weather affects the route, Purge Pros will communicate the updated plan.
