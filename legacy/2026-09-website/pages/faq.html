Getting Started

Coverage, access, and what happens after you submit a service request.

Start here
How do I know whether you service my address?

Start with the ZIP-code check in our quote tool. If your ZIP is covered, you can continue through pricing and submit your service request.

Because recurring service is route-based, our local team still reviews the full address and current route capacity before confirming your service day. We would rather confirm a dependable route than promise a day we cannot support.

Do I need to be home during service?

No. As long as the service areas are safely accessible and you provide any gate, pet, or access instructions, you can go about your day.

  • Before we arrive

    You receive a heads-up text when the technician is on the way.

  • After service

    You receive a gate-closure photo after the visit is completed.

  • Access notes

    We follow the approved instructions saved for your property and pets.

What happens after I request recurring service?

Submitting the form requests service; it does not instantly assign a route day. Our local team reviews your address, selected plan, and current route capacity.

After your route is approved and a service day is confirmed, we send your service details and a secure Housecall Pro card-on-file link. We contact you using the preference selected in the quote tool.

Plans & Billing

Contracts, per-visit billing, payment, and one-time cleanup details.

Pricing
Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. There is no minimum number of visits and no cancellation fee. You pay for completed visits, and cancelling does not trigger repayment of a waived initial scoop surcharge.

Scheduled recurring visits stay at your quoted rate regardless of cleanup time. After a long pause or repeated customer-requested skips, a restart cleanup may be needed: your quoted visit rate includes 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. We confirm any restart pricing before booking.

How do billing and payment work?

Recurring service is pay per visit, not a flat monthly bill. After route approval, you receive a secure link to place a major credit or debit card on file.

Your visit rate or applicable base deposit is charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent. For standard initial/restart and one-time cleanups, any applicable extra time is invoiced after completion. The base counts toward that cleanup; it is not added a second time.

Approved promotional first cleanups and scheduled recurring visits have no additional-time charge. Receipts are provided electronically. We do not accept cash or checks left at the property.

Do you offer one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. Select One-Time Cleanup in the quote tool to request service.

The $89.99 base deposit is charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent. Any additional time is invoiced after completion. The deposit is credited toward the total, not charged again.

The new recurring-customer offer does not apply to one-time cleanups.

How does the initial scoop surcharge waiver work?

Eligible new recurring customers pay only their quoted regular visit rate for the entire first cleanup, including built-up poop and all extra time. Following scheduled recurring visits also stay at the quoted rate, regardless of time spent.

Without the offer, a standard initial or restart cleanup includes 30 minutes at your quoted visit rate, then $1 per actual additional minute. This is the charge for that cleanup, not an extra base fee on top of another visit charge.

For example, a 60-minute initial cleanup normally costs your visit rate plus $30. With the offer, you pay only your visit rate, saving $30. Actual savings are $1 per minute beyond 30; a cleanup of 30 minutes or less has no time surcharge to waive.

One introductory offer per customer/household, for new recurring service only. We confirm service history and any custom scope before booking. One-time cleanups are excluded. No minimum visits, cancellation fee or repayment of the waived surcharge.

What yard size should I enter for my quote?

Estimate the total outdoor area that needs scooping, including side yards, dog runs, and patios or concrete where your dogs go. Leave out the house and areas that do not need scooping.

Pricing uses dog count, visit frequency and the requested cleanup area. If that area exceeds one acre or the work falls outside standard options, use Custom Estimate for an estimate. A larger property does not automatically need a custom quote if the area needing scooping is smaller.

Service Day & Safety

Who visits your yard, how access is handled, and what happens in bad weather.

Service day
Are you insured, and who performs the service?

Yes. Purge Pros carries business insurance, and field service is performed by background-checked W-2 employees trained to our service and property-care standards—not random gig workers.

You receive a notification before arrival, and the person servicing your property is identifiable as part of the Purge Pros team.

How do you handle pets, gates, and property access?

We save your approved pet, gate, and property-access instructions so the technician knows how to enter and secure the service area.

  • Gate security

    After the visit, we close the gate and send a photo showing it secured.

  • Pet notes

    Treats are provided only when owner-approved, and allergy or no-treat instructions are documented.

  • Sanitation

    Tools and footwear are treated between yards to reduce cross-property contamination.

  • Unsafe conditions

    Reactive, protective, or escape-risk dogs must remain inside. We may pause a visit if safe service is not possible.

Do you provide service year-round and during bad weather?

Yes. We work through ordinary rain, cold, and snow when the yard can be serviced safely. Lightning, flooding, unsafe ice, extreme cold, or another serious hazard may delay or pause service.

If weather forces a change, we send an update and try to reschedule. If Purge Pros cancels and cannot reschedule that visit, you are not charged; the accumulated waste is addressed on the next scheduled visit without an extra accumulation charge.

Schedule Changes

What to expect when you need to skip, pause, or request another service day.

Changes
Can I skip, pause, or reschedule a visit?

Yes. Contact us as early as possible, preferably at least 48 hours before your route day. We will try to accommodate another day, but a specific alternate day cannot be guaranteed.

If your dogs are away and no new waste is accumulating, let us know. If waste continues to accumulate during a long pause or repeated customer-requested skips, we may need a restart cleanup: your quoted visit rate includes 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. We confirm any restart pricing before booking; applicable extra time is invoiced after completion.

Ordinary scheduled recurring visits have no time surcharge. A Purge Pros weather cancellation is not treated as a customer skip; accumulated waste is handled on the next scheduled visit without an extra accumulation charge.
