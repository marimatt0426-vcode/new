Questions before you start?

Clear answers before you request Brownsburg service.

Does a Brownsburg ZIP confirm a recurring service day?

No. A supported ZIP opens the price check. Purge Pros reviews the exact address, requested scope, and current route capacity before a recurring day is offered.

What should I include in my request?

Include the dogs using the yard, total outdoor area to scoop, every front, back, side, patio, or concrete area needing service, preferred frequency, and gate or access details.

What starting prices should I compare?

The starting example for one dog, one service area, and up to one-eighth acre is $19.99 per weekly visit. Twice weekly starts at $15.99 per visit for the same example. Your selected scope determines the available price or custom estimate.

Can I request a one-time cleanup?

Yes. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per additional minute. The new recurring-customer offer does not apply to one-time cleanups.

What if a Brownsburg yard has not been cleaned recently?

Tell us approximately when the yard was last cleaned so we can plan the visit. Eligible new recurring customers pay only their quoted regular visit rate for the entire first cleanup, including built-up waste and all extra time. Standard initial or restart cleanups include 30 minutes at the quoted visit rate, then $1 per actual additional minute. We confirm the applicable pricing before booking.

Is a one-time Brownsburg cleanup priced like recurring service?

No. One-time cleanup is $89.99 for the first 30 minutes, then $1 per actual additional minute. The $89.99 base deposit is charged when your on-the-way ETA text is sent; additional time is invoiced after completion. The deposit is credited toward the total, not charged again. The new recurring-customer offer does not apply.

What is included with recurring dog waste removal?

Recurring visits include a systematic search of the selected service areas, removal and haul-away of the collected dog waste, sanitized tools between properties, arrival communication, and the gate-security routine.

How does the initial scoop surcharge waiver work?

New recurring customers pay their quoted regular visit price for the entire first cleanup, with the initial scoop surcharge waived. Without the offer, an initial cleanup includes 30 minutes at that rate, then $1 per additional minute. A 60-minute cleanup saves $30 with the offer; actual savings vary, and a cleanup of 30 minutes or less has no time surcharge to waive. Scheduled recurring visits have no time surcharge. One offer per customer/household; one-time cleanups excluded. We confirm service history and any custom scope before booking. No minimum visits or cancellation fee.

How is the per-visit price calculated?

The quote uses dog count, requested frequency, and the total outdoor area to scoop, including any patio or concrete needing service. Leave out the house and areas that do not need scooping. Larger or more complex properties may require a custom estimate instead of an instant price.
